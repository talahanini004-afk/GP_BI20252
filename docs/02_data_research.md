# Data Research and Acquiring Effort

## Data Sources

The data used in this project was collected from official Jordanian sources:

1. Jordan Department of Statistics (DOS)
2. Social Security Corporation (SSC)
3. Ministry of Education (MoE)

## Source Descriptions

The Jordan Department of Statistics was used as the main source for labor market and population-related data, including unemployment rate, employment rate, labor force indicators, population distribution, and governorate-level demographic data.

The Social Security Corporation was used to obtain data related to the number of establishments. Establishments were important because they represent economic activity and potential job creation.

The Ministry of Education was used to obtain education-related indicators, including number of schools, number of students, number of classes, students per class, and schools per 1,000 population.

## Data Selection Criteria

The dataset was selected based on:

- Relevance to unemployment analysis.
- Availability at governorate level.
- Coverage across multiple years.
- Compatibility with panel data analysis.
- Reliability of the source.
- Suitability for regression, clustering, and visualization.
